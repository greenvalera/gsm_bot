<objective>
Make the day step complete: all seven days of the target week, the two highlight
markers with their legend, the default-wins-on-a-tie rule, and the visible-marked-
refused treatment of days already in the past.

Purpose: PLAN-04 and PLAN-05 are the phase's most visible product surface and the
place 02-CONTEXT.md's organizing idea lives — "the chat's configured window should
look the same on a Thursday as on a Monday". Hiding past days would make the card's
shape change with the day of the week, which the CONTEXT explicitly rejects.

Output: a day card an author can read at a glance and act on, with the marker rules
and the keyboard row split pinned by unit tests so neither can regress silently.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Telegram group -> day-button callback | Any member can tap any day button, including a stale one on a card that has sat in the chat across midnight. |
| Render time -> tap time | The two are separated by arbitrary wall-clock delay; nothing rendered may be trusted as still true at tap time. |
