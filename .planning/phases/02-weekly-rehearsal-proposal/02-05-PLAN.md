<objective>
Close the wizard: Back on every step after the first, a review step that shows exactly
what is about to be committed and who will be asked, and a Confirm that atomically
promotes the draft into the durable proposal Phase 3 will read.

Purpose: D-04 makes this the phase's terminal state — "no placeholder or disabled
publish control is shipped". D-09's organizing principle lands here too: the active
roster IS the lineup, there is no participant-selection step, and changing who plays
is changing the roster. The Confirm transaction is where all four of those decisions
become one atomic durable fact.

Output: a proposal the band has actually agreed on, snapshotted and committed, with
the week released for the next round.
</objective>
