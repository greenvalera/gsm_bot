<objective>
Announce that the rehearsal is ready to book the moment every included participant has answered can-attend, exactly once per achieved unanimity, and keep that announcement honest when a participant later changes their mind.

Purpose: this is the one moment in the round that has to break through, and it is also the one moment two concurrent taps can double. The claim has to be a database compare-and-set, and — per the owner's binding decision — it has to be a rate-limited one rather than a one-shot, so a slot that becomes workable again can still be announced.
Output: `READY_ANNOUNCE_COOLDOWN_MS` and the `readyAnnouncedAt` claim, `recordAnnouncement`, `renderReadyAnnouncement` / `renderRetractedAnnouncement`, `editRoundMessage` extracted from `editAnchor`, the post / edit / retract branches on the answer dispatch, and the ready-state `/plan_status` re-post.
</objective>

## Recorded decisions

**D-17 — the round carries two durable message ids, and `anchorMessageId` does not move.**
D-12 says the announcement "becomes the round's new anchor". That is implemented *behaviorally*: from the moment the announcement exists it is the message that carries the round's remaining control (plan 03-05) and the message `/plan_status` re-posts. The `anchorMessageId` column keeps naming the availability card, because RESEARCH Pitfall 7 requires that card to stay live and editable for the whole round (D-04 keeps answers changeable until booking closes it) and `anchorMessageId` is the only durable handle on it — repurposing the column would leave the card unreachable and permanently stale. `announcementMessageId` names the announcement. Reversibility: **costly** — a later phase that wants a single moving anchor needs a third column for the card, or a migration.

**Owner acknowledgement.** D-17 reinterprets a locked decision, so it is not the planner's to make alone. It was put to the owner during `/gsd-plan-phase 3` plan verification and **accepted**. The acceptance is recorded as an amendment appended to D-12 in `.planning/phases/03-availability-and-booking-decision/03-CONTEXT.md`, which supersedes D-12's original reversibility note where the two conflict. **Phase 4's LIFE-02 and LIFE-05 must read the amended shape, not D-12's original text.** Nothing in this plan's design changes as a result — the amendment records what 03-04 and 03-05 already implement.

**D-18 — re-announce, but rate-limited (owner decision, binding; overrides RESEARCH Open Question 1 recommendation (c)).**
`readyAnnouncedAt` is not a one-shot claim for the life of the round. It is claimed by a cooldown-style compare-and-set in the same shape as the shipped `lastStatusPostedAt` claim, and it is **never nulled** — the passage of `READY_ANNOUNCE_COOLDOWN_MS` *is* the release. Inside the window a re-achieved unanimity edits the existing announcement (notifies nobody); outside it, a fresh message is posted (notifies). Halves (a) and (b) of the researcher's recommendation stand: the booking apply re-derives unanimity (plan 03-05), and a lost unanimity edits the announcement to a retraction. Reversibility: **reversible** — a constant and a predicate.

**The constant** — in `src/domain/planning/planning-service.ts`, beside `PLANNING_STATUS_COOLDOWN_MS`, add `export const READY_ANNOUNCE_COOLDOWN_MS = 30 * 60 * 1000;`. It is a separate constant with a separate value on purpose: the status cooldown rate-limits a command anyone can spam, and one minute is right for that; this one rate-limits a group notification that fires from an ordinary participant's mis-tap, where a minute would let a flip-flopping tap notify the band repeatedly within a single conversation. Thirty minutes is short enough that a slot which genuinely becomes workable again later in the day still breaks through. Do not reuse and do not change `PLANNING_STATUS_COOLDOWN_MS`.

**The claim** — inside `answerAvailability`'s existing `$transaction`, after the participant re-read that produces the `answered` result, derive the outcome with `availabilityOutcome(participants)` (the one derivation function; never re-derive at a call site) and pick a directive:

1. Outcome is the all-available value → attempt `tx.planningRound.updateMany({ where: { id: round.id, status: PlanningRoundStatus.CONFIRMED, OR: [{ readyAnnouncedAt: null }, { readyAnnouncedAt: { lte: cutoff } }] }, data: { readyAnnouncedAt: now } })` where `cutoff` is `new Date(now.getTime() - READY_ANNOUNCE_COOLDOWN_MS)`. `count === 1` → directive `post`. `count !== 1` → directive `edit` when the round already has an `announcementMessageId`, otherwise `none`.
2. Outcome is not the all-available value → directive `retract` when the round has both a non-null `readyAnnouncedAt` and a non-null `announcementMessageId`, otherwise `none`.

Copy the `OR` form from the shipped `lastStatusPostedAt` claim verbatim in shape. It is the whole guarantee: two concurrent final answers both reach this statement, the second blocks on the row lock and re-evaluates against the row the first committed, and exactly one sees one affected row. An application-level test of a previously-read value posts twice. (The owner's decision wrote the predicate with a strict inequality; the shipped house form uses the inclusive one and the difference is a single-millisecond boundary with no behavioral consequence — match the house form.)

Do NOT bump `revision` in this claim, for the reason the status claim gives: an announcement is not a step transition, and bumping would make one participant's tap invalidate another's in-flight action. Do NOT null `readyAnnouncedAt` anywhere — the column is simultaneously the claim and the record of when the last announcement happened, and the cooldown is what releases it. Return the directive on the `answered` member of `AnswerResult` as an `AnnouncementDirective` union.

**The renderer** — in `src/telegram/planning-renderers.ts`, add `renderReadyAnnouncement(projection, controlTokens)` returning the same `RenderedStep` shape as the card. Its keyboard comes from `planningControlRows(PLANNING_BOOKING_ROWS, controlTokens)`; in this plan every caller passes a lookup that answers undefined, so no keyboard is rendered. Plan 03-05 mints the booking control and the same call starts producing one — the signature does not change between plans. Never a Telegram mention (D-10). Keep the renderer a total function of its projection: no I/O, no clock, no minting.
