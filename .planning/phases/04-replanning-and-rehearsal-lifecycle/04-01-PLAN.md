Land the durable cancellation/supersession schema and prove the phase's spine end to end: one
"Cannot attend" closes the round, and the eligible actor turns that blocked round into a fresh
day selector in one transaction.

(blocked announcement, cancel, change, lifecycle defaults) either extends this transaction or
depends on the enum label this migration adds. Proving the whole path on one thin slice catches
an architectural dead-end after one commit rather than after four.
Output: A migration plus regenerated client, and a working replan path from tap to new card.
</objective>
