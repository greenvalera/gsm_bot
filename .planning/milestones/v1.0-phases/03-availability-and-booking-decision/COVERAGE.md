# API Coverage — Telegram Bot API and grammY (Phase 3)

> Full coverage by default. This matrix enumerates the **Phase 3 availability and booking surface** — what availability collection and the manual booking decision add on top of Phase 2. Every subtraction is an explicit, reasoned opt-out.
>
> Phases 1 and 2 remain in force and are not restated. Phase 2's closing boundary required exactly this extension once a phase added "per-participant response buttons, non-responder follow-ups, or any direct-message surface," and named three opt-outs as most likely to flip: *direct messages to participants*, *`sendChatAction`*, and *scheduled delivery*. **None of the three flipped** — the reasons below say why, and Phase 5 is where two of them come back.

> Phase 3 adds no external service, SDK, or provider, and no npm root (`03-RESEARCH.md` → Package Legitimacy Audit: "Packages installed by this phase: none"). ## Notes on the load-bearing rows

**The round now owns two messages, and that is the structural change.** Phase 2's rule was "exactly one live card." Phase 3 keeps that rule per *slot* and adds a second slot (D-17): the anchor carries the availability card, and the announcement carries the ready-to-book claim and the `Mark as booked` control. Every correction path names its slot explicitly — `retractStaleAnnouncement`, the cooldown edit, and `closeBookedRound` all address `announcementMessageId` alone, which is why an announcement the round failed to record a pointer to has to be stripped of its buttons rather than left live (the G-03 compensation at `planning-handlers.ts:2150-2200`).

**The availability card is deliberately not touched when the announcement changes.** Answers stay changeable until booking closes the round (D-04), and the two keyboards address different durable rows, so they cannot race. A retraction corrects the announcement and leaves the answer controls live.

**Bounding an alert is a Telegram-unit problem, not a string problem.** Telegram counts `answerCallbackQuery` text in UTF-16 code units. Measuring a display name in anything else lets a name built from astral-plane characters satisfy the budget and still be rejected on the wire — which is why `boundedLabel` compares `String#length` and cuts on the same unit.

## Coverage Boundary

This matrix covers every Telegram/grammY capability Phase 3's availability collection and booking decision uses or plausibly reaches for. **Phase 4 (replanning and rehearsal lifecycle) must extend it again** when it adds cancellation, change, or completed-rehearsal surfaces — LIFE-03 and LIFE-04 ship no control in Phase 3, and "Not yet" is a decline, not an undo. **Phase 5 (proactive reminders) is where the three opt-outs above most plausibly flip**: non-responder follow-ups and scheduled delivery both arrive there, the latter as a pg-boss-driven `sendMessage` rather than a Telegram-side schedule.

**Not covered by any test in this repository:** what a Telegram *client* draws — marker glyph availability, button label truncation at real device widths, and the visual result of editing one of a pair of messages in place. That is why `03-UAT.md` exists and cannot be discharged by adding tests here.
