## Assumption-delta decision (advisory, non-blocking)

The detector fired `kind: chosen` on the ROADMAP phrase "publish a **custom** availability card". Reviewed against the phase scope, the genuine identity change is not that word but this: `PlanningRound.status` gains a fourth value while the round simultaneously gains a second live message.

- **Primary noun:** `PlanningRound.status` — the single durable lifecycle position of a round.
- **Decision: `promote`.** `BOOKED` is added to the status enum (D-15) so the compiler exhausts the lifecycle, rather than adding a nullable `bookedAt` flag alongside a still-`CONFIRMED` status. `bookedAt` exists only as the timestamp detail of that status, never as the authority for "is it booked".
- **Corollary:** `anchorMessageId` stays the round's single *live control surface*; `announcementMessageId` (D-12) is a detail of one lifecycle position, not a second parallel anchor concept.
- **Suggested invariant test (accepted, lands in plan 03-04):** every round that reaches `BOOKED` round-trips through `status`, never through a `bookedAt !== null` check at a call site.
