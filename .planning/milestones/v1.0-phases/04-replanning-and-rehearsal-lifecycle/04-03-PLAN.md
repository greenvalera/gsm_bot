<objective>
Deliver LIFE-03 and the release half of LIFE-06: the planning author or a chat administrator can cancel
an active or booked rehearsal, behind a named confirmation, and the week goes back to the chat.

Purpose: cancellation is the transition with no undo, and it is also the one that turns the enum label
plan 04-01 added into a reachable state. Doing it after the replan spine means the confirm-then-apply
gate, the control-bearing-message rule, and the terminal render all have a proven precedent to copy.
Output: `/plan_cancel` and an inline cancel control, both landing on one guarded transition, plus the
D-19 authorization widening that keeps the new cancelled position from revoking anyone's planning access.

Note on scope: Task 1 carries a two-line edit to `wasPreviousParticipant()` that reads like plan 04-05's
work. It is here on purpose. This plan is the one that makes the cancelled position reachable, and the
instant it does, that function's status filter starts revoking `PREVIOUS_PARTICIPANTS` standing when a
chat's only rehearsal is cancelled. The write that opens the hazard and the read that closes it ship in
one commit, so no intermediate state of `master` — including one reached by a mid-phase halt — carries
the regression.
</objective>
