<decision_record>
**Same-week scope for `/plan_change` — resolving the discretion item CONTEXT.md handed to research
and planning.**

`/plan_change` operates **only within the round's own target week**. The successor's `targetWeekStart`
is copied verbatim from the round being changed; a change never names a different week.

Why: the confirm transition performs no week-claim check — its validations are the token, the status,
the step, week-date membership, slot membership and slot availability. The week claim is checked in
exactly one place, the planning-start path. A cross-week change would therefore be the first path in
the system to create a draft for a week without going through that check, and it would still leave a
window until confirm that no database constraint closes. Same-week date and time changes satisfy
LIFE-04's literal text, keep change byte-identical to replan as D-12 requires, and avoid opening an
unguarded week claim.

Rejected alternative and its cost: allowing a cross-week change would require adding a week-claim check
to the shipped confirm transition — a Phase 4 scope expansion touching a transition Phase 2 shipped and
Phase 3 depends on. A band that needs a different week already has a clean, fully specified two-step
path: `/plan_cancel` frees the week (LIFE-06, automatic under D-13), then `/plan` rolls to the right
week (D-18, delivered in plan 04-05).
</decision_record>
