<objective>
Make the round survivable: ownership that refuses politely and names who owns it, a
status request that brings the live card back to the bottom of the chat where people
are actually looking, resumption of the exact in-flight state after a restart, and
administrator takeover of a round whose author has gone quiet.

Purpose: this is the half of the phase goal the word "recover" carries. PLAN-10 and
RELI-01 are what make the wizard usable in a real group chat where the card gets
buried under conversation and the bot gets redeployed mid-week; AUTH-03 is what stops
one member's abandoned round holding the whole band's week hostage. 02-RESEARCH.md
Pattern 8 establishes that none of this needs a scheduler: lazy evaluation on
`lastActivityAt` at read time covers both abandonment and stale-week reaping, and
pg-boss stays in Phase 5 where it belongs.

Output: a round that can be found, resumed, and — only when genuinely abandoned —
handed to an administrator, without a background job anywhere in the phase.
</objective>

<stated_assumptions>
- **Assumption A2 (adopted):** the abandonment threshold is 30 minutes,
  `PLANNING_INACTIVITY_MS = 30 * 60 * 1000`, mirroring the Phase 1 `DRAFT_LIFETIME_MS`
  and `ROSTER_ACTION_LIFETIME_MS` constants. The constant was declared in 02-02 and is
  consumed here. It is stated aloud because CONTEXT granted discretion but the number is
  load-bearing: too short lets an administrator seize a round from an author who stepped
  out for coffee, too long makes AUTH-03 unusable.
- **Assumption A7 (adopted):** the status re-post cooldown is short and on the order of
  30-60 seconds; `PLANNING_STATUS_COOLDOWN_MS` is declared in this plan. D-15 opens the
  status request to everyone in the chat, so without a cooldown it is an unrated flood
  vector (02-RESEARCH.md Pitfall 8).
- **Assumption A6 (adopted, cosmetic — CONTEXT grants discretion):** the status command
  is `/plan_status`.
- **No scheduler is introduced.** Abandonment is evaluated lazily at read time and
  stale-week reaping happens on the read path. Introducing `pg-boss` here would add a
  queue schema, a worker lifecycle and a dependency gate for a problem that needs no
  background job at all; `.claude/CLAUDE.md` assigns pg-boss to the deferred-jobs phase.
  An in-process `setInterval` or `setTimeout` reaper is explicitly forbidden — it does
  not survive restarts and cannot coordinate replicas, which is the exact reason the
  project rejects `node-cron` and `setTimeout` for scheduling.
</stated_assumptions>
